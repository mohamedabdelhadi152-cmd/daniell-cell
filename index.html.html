ELECTROCHEMISTRY • FOUR-STAGE EXPLAINER

Inside the Daniell Cell

Follow the atoms, ions, and electrons as a chemical reaction generates electric current.

OXIDATION • AT THE ZINC ANODE

Zn(s) → Zn²⁺(aq) + 2e⁻

REDUCTION • AT THE COPPER CATHODE

Cu²⁺(aq) + 2e⁻ → Cu(s)

01 · Zinc atoms, Zn(s), lose two electrons each. Zinc ions, Zn²⁺, enter the solution, so the zinc anode gradually corrodes, becomes thinner, and loses mass.

02 · Electrons (e⁻) released at the zinc anode travel through the external wire and voltmeter toward the copper cathode. The zinc strip continues to lose mass.

03 · Copper ions, Cu²⁺, gain arriving electrons at the copper cathode. Solid Cu(s) deposits directly on its surface, making the copper strip thicker and heavier in place.

04 · The Na₂SO₄ salt bridge completes the circuit: sulfate ions (SO₄²⁻) migrate toward the Zn²⁺-rich anode half-cell, while sodium ions (Na⁺) migrate toward the cathode half-cell to maintain electrical neutrality.
